A SMALL GUIDE

Hello, Cloudkeeper.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys. On a touch screen, use the arrows or tap the grass.

Look for the little ✦ signs. Walk over and press E, or tap a sign to help.

Each island is one maths level. Open the Island map to see all twelve. Collect six rescue stars to unlock the next island. Three successful answers restore its landmark. Use a hint or choose a gentler difficulty whenever you like.

Every player has separate progress for each school year. Edit a nickname or add a player on the title screen. Questions get harder after two independent answers and gentler after two missed questions. Mastered levels stay open for practice. Continue returns to your saved island and question. Guest saves stay in this browser. Parent sign in enables online saves when configured. Download a backup to keep or transfer them. There’s no timer.
